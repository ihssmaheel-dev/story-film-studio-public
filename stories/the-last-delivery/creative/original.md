## The Last Delivery

Crime Thriller | Suspense | 1-Minute Story

At 2:13 AM, a drug dealer named Leo received a message from an unknown number.

"One last delivery. $100,000. No questions."

Leo had been dealing drugs for seven years. He'd never seen an offer like this.

The pickup was simple. A black suitcase, waiting inside an abandoned warehouse.

He grabbed it and drove to the delivery address.

But when he arrived, his hands began shaking.

It was his childhood home.

The house had been empty for fifteen years.

His phone buzzed.

"Go inside."

Leo slowly opened the front door.

In the living room sat an old television, playing a recording.

It showed Leo, seven years earlier, making his very first drug deal.

Then another recording.

And another.

Every crime he'd ever committed.

Suddenly, the television went black.

White letters appeared.

"OPEN THE SUITCASE."

Leo unlatched it.

There were no drugs.

Only a photograph of his younger brother, who had died from an overdose three years ago.

Behind the photograph was a note.

"You sold him his first dose."

Leo froze.

His phone rang.

He answered with trembling hands.

A familiar voice whispered:

"Hello, big brother."

Then the lights went out.

And someone upstairs started walking.

THE END.
