This is an unfilled template, not proof that a review occurred. Populate using root `instructions/story-audit.md` before offering corrections. Original source remains unchanged.

## A. Overall Assessment

Scope, evidenced strengths, coherence, clarity, continuity and editorial readiness.

## B. Critical Issues

Confirmed critical findings, or an explicit statement that none were identified within the completed scope.

## C. Complete Issue List

For each stable AUDIT ID: classification, severity, location/quote, exact problem, conflicting/supporting evidence, assumptions/confidence, impact, smallest recommended fix/example, alternatives and affected scenes/entities. Sort confirmed issues by severity. Clearly separate possible/unverified findings; do not invent criticism.

## D. Scene-by-Scene Analysis

Every scene in source order: what works, linked issues, missing context, dialogue/format, and connection to the next scene. Reconcile counts with `scene-map.md`.

## E. Global Continuity Report

Chronology; character knowledge/motives; locations/travel; objects/injuries/evidence; relationships/arcs; rules/factual checks; setups/clues/revelations/payoffs. Link scene/ledger evidence.

## F. Unresolved Questions

Material author decisions/omissions, verification needs and relevant unanswered intake constraints. Separate intentional ambiguity.

## G. Target Runtime and Part Structure Assessment

User's single-video/part targets, flexibility, justified duration range/confidence, scene pacing, proposed split points and conflicts/tradeoffs. No fixed runtime assumption.

## H. Prioritized Correction Checklist

Essential fixes in dependency order, then optional improvements. List IDs/scope and decisions required before any edit.

## I. Final Verdict

NOT READY / NEEDS REVISION / NEARLY PRODUCTION-READY / NO SIGNIFICANT ISSUES FOUND, with scope/evidence/limitations. This is an editorial verdict, not G1–G6 or production authorization.

After presenting this report, ask permission for specific fixes/revision and record the decision. Do not automatically rewrite.
