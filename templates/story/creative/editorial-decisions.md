# Editorial permission and decisions

Draft. Preserve the original. Record the actual configuration authorization for delegated minor edits, or issue-specific permission before a non-delegated/major edit. Major changes always need a decision first. A report, silence or a preference is not permission.

| Decision | Source/finding revision | Issue IDs and exact scope | Protected choices | Actual quotation/reference | Timestamp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Fast revisions also use `studio record-revision` to bind the complete revised bytes and actual permission/delegation. Strict follows the full report, fix permission, complete revision/recheck and separate G1 sequence.
