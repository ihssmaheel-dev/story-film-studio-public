# 02 — Story review, part structure and pacing

> **Workflow:** Fast is the new-project default; see [Fast workflow](13-fast-workflow.md). G1–G6 and separate reference/audition signoffs in this guide describe Strict mode. Fast uses scoped delegation and internal review; technical, continuity and genuine QA requirements still apply. Existing projects without a workflow record remain Strict.

## Diagnose before editing

Use the active audit mode: Fast reads the entire work, maps every scene, checks all twelve areas and cross-checks risky details; Full/Strict follows the complete second-pass procedure in `instructions/story-audit.md`. Strict delivers the complete A–I report before fix permission. Fast presents meaningful findings concisely and applies only the actually delegated minor corrections; major changes need permission. Proposed examples are not edits. Preserve author intent, effective dialogue, deliberate ambiguity and ending. Judge rough prose at its own level instead of imposing screenplay conventions.

## Whole-story planning

After the editorial decision, record logline, goals/flaws, antagonistic pressure, inciting incident, escalating choices, reversals, low point, climax, resolution, theme and emotional change. Separate audience knowledge from character knowledge; link later reveals to earlier clues/props. The audit ledger is evidence for production continuity, not automatic approved canon.

Genre/theme, setting and visual style are independent. A chosen look does not imply a language, culture, plot formula or different ending. Important reveal details must remain readable in that style.

## One video or meaningful parts

Reuse a stated output choice. A full-length/single-film request uses only `part_001`, with a complete beginning, dramatic progression, climax and intended ending. Ask only missing duration/flexibility before G2 or the Fast configuration. For part-by-part delivery, record per-part targets/ranges, flexibility, desired count/total and meaningful boundaries. New multipart configuration includes the individual videos and a full-film cut unless parts only was requested. Recommend lengths with narrative reasons when asked. Never assume a duration from an example or force a default count.

Write the complete multipart story as labeled part sections in the authoritative final story file before generating production assets. Preserve the full source and authorized plot/ending; presentation into parts does not authorize new twists or a changed ending. Each part needs an engaging opening grounded in the actual scene, a local dramatic question, meaningful escalation/change and an earned ending. Longer parts may contain several sequences. A nonfinal ending provides local satisfaction or a meaningful turn and creates a concrete reason to continue: a consequence, decision, revelation, unresolved goal or earned cliffhanger. Record what the next part owes the audience and where it pays off; its opening must connect to the preceding ending without replaying the whole part. The final part pays off the overall story and reaches its intended ending. Avoid filler, arbitrary cutoffs, empty teasers and mandatory short-video formulas.

`creative/part-plan.md` records mode/outputs, exact final-story part headings, duration policy, part IDs/titles, start/end story states, target/range, opening hook/purpose, central question, scene IDs/beats, clues, decisions, visual/sound reveals, earned endings/continuation promises, next-part payoff points, estimated shots and dependencies. Fast may detail written shots progressively after the complete part narratives and boundaries are established, producing parts in order with the same canon. A single-video plan has no next-part promise or separate multipart cut.

## Runtime and shot rhythm

Estimate a range using performed speech, action, reactions, silence, transitions and scene complexity, not words/pages alone. Later measure real recordings. Discuss scope/pacing/duration conflicts instead of padding or unnatural speed changes. Choose still counts and holds beat by beat; longer parts need deliberate visual development, not a fixed set of images held indefinitely.

The AI also chooses each hold's camera treatment for its story purpose: static for observation or stillness, a restrained push toward a meaningful reaction/clue, a pull back to reveal context, or a pan connecting details. Use [camera motion](12-camera-motion.md) and include the exact plan in G4. Preserve reveal timing and intentional pauses. A camera move does not substitute for a new image when action, state or viewpoint needs to change.

Write speakable language suited to the agreed language/tone, concrete detail, varied sentences and purposeful pauses. Keep pronunciations consistent. Reading-rate estimates are planning aids, never alignment. Sound follows the scene's physical world/emotional arc, including silence.

## Quality and continuous editions

Assess comprehension, causal logic, motivations, emotional investment, freshness, stakes, visual clarity, payoff and full resolution with evidence. Scores alone do not prove quality. Resolve material weaknesses before G2/G4.

When `continuous_edition` is true in multipart intake, completing the full film is part of the requested delivery. Keep clean stems and a measured edit decision list in `creative/continuous-edit.md`; build the edition under `full-film/`. Remove duplicated release recaps/teasers/outros and repeated titles, preserving hooks that are real story events. Rebuild joins and one continuous subtitle/audio clock, then render, preview and inspect the complete full film separately. Reuse canonical characters and existing story assets. Do not leave a promised full cut as a future suggestion or claim that concatenating parts alone completed its edit/QA.
